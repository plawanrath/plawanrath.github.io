Cross-Dialect Generalization Without Retraining: Benchmarks and Evaluation of Schema-Derived Constrained Decoding for MLIR

NeurIPS 2026 · Evaluations & Datasets Track

Training-free, ODS-aware constrained decoding lets small language models generate MLIR across dialects — arith, func, memref, linalg, and StableHLO — without retraining. The release includes six public benchmarks (410 in-scope NL→MLIR pairs plus held-out and out-of-grammar suites) with validated Croissant metadata.

Quantization Undoes Alignment

IEEE Cloud Summit 2026

Does post-training quantization amplify social bias? This study quantizes three instruction-tuned models from BF16 down to 3-bit and measures stereotype reliance on the BBQ benchmark across five bias categories. All 15 MLX-format quantized artifacts are published.

arXiv:2605.15208 Code: compression-bias-amplification Models on Hugging Face

Weight Pruning Amplifies Bias

AIIoT 2026

How does weight pruning change bias in instruction-tuned LLMs headed for edge deployment? A 3 × 3 × 4 grid — three base models, three pruning methods (random, magnitude, Wanda), four sparsities — with all 36 pruned checkpoints released for reproducibility.

arXiv:2605.08137 Code: pruning-impact-analysis Models on Hugging Face

Knowledge Distillation Impact Analysis

arXiv 2026

How do response-based, logit-based, and combined knowledge distillation affect bias in small instruction-tuned models? Students (SmolLM2, OLMo-2) are distilled from Gemma-2, Mistral, and Phi teachers and evaluated on BBQ and CrowS-Pairs, including refusal-calibration and mitigation analysis.
